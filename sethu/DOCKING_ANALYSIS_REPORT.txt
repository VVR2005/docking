7. KEY FINDINGS / ISSUES
------------------------------------------------------------------------
[ISSUE 1] Ki miscalculation in docking_summary.txt
  - The file reports Ki = 1.16e-11 M (11 pM).
  - For dG = -5.95 kcal/mol, the correct Ki (Ki = exp(dG/RT),
    RT = 0.592 kcal/mol at 298 K) is approximately 43 uM.
  - A pM Ki would require dG ~ -15 kcal/mol.
  - RECOMMENDATION: correct the Ki value in the summary file.

[ISSUE 2] Grid center vs. actual pose location
  - Declared grid center : (21.965, 6.046, 57.018)
  - Best-pose coordinates : ~(27.0, -1.0, 53.0)
  - The ligand docked ~5-7 A away from the grid center.
  - RECOMMENDATION: verify the grid center correctly encloses the
    ATP-binding pocket before re-running.

[ISSUE 3] Receptor preparation validation
  - Original 1M17.pdb contains:
      * Ligand chain '4' (831 atoms)   <- additional/alternate chain
      * 29-atom HETATM erlotinib (AQ4)
      * 20 water molecules (HOH)
  - The clean step removed hydrogens/water/inhibitor; confirm the
    co-crystallized erlotinib was truly removed so docking targets the
    intended active site.

[ISSUE 4] Weak affinity & limited chemical space
  - Phthalazine is a small rigid scaffold with no H-bond donors.
  - -5.95 kcal/mol reflects fragment-level binding.
